Mariam Mustazaf

This is Mariam Mustazaf, an Afghan youth passionate about Medical Data Science and promoting women's rights in Afghanistan.

Biography
  • Born in summar of 2005 in Afghanistan a country of rich culture and history; I grew up amid the contrasting beauty of ancient traditions and modern challenges. From an early age, I have been driven to learn, and discover knowledge through science and mathematics; making me a strong candidate to receive a scholarship in Afghan-Turk Maarif Schools, Kabul Girls High School among thousands of girls applying for the program in 2019.
  • "Every catastrophe is also an opportunity." This quote perfectly encapsulates my journey and my passion for perseverance when schools got banned for girls beyond grade six in 2021, I chose to focus on the bright side of this tragic situation.
  • Therefore, I became one of the top 10 nominees for the 2021 Children’s Peace Prize among candidates from around the world for the recognition of my voluntary efforts to educate and mentor Afghan women and children. Over seven years of my volunteer jobs (2019-2026), I provided educational support, mentoring, and tutoring to over 1000 women at the Khadija Kubra Islamic Educational Center, the Lincoln Learning Center, and via home-schooling programs.
Background
  • Throughout my academic and volunteer journey, I have worked with several national and international organizations, expanding my knowledge in order to help Afghan women and children.
  • In January of 2024, I joined the Amala learning bridge program for raising awareness about mental illness through online platforms. In result, we were able to help and raise awareness among 500+ Afghan women in our 3 months seminars.
  • Later, I co-founded the Parvaaz Women’s Mental Health Support Foundation and continued our journey to support Afghan women mental health by providing weekly and monthly online sessions, reading book sessions, an online library, and one-on-one therapist support for over 700+ girls through our 8- month journey.
  • Moreover, being accepted in Alekain foundation become life-changing opportunity for me, enabling me pursue my educational journey, and bringing me closer to my greatest ambition, becoming one of the first medical data scientist in Afghanistan.
  • As a dedicated and goal-oriented Afghan girl who has devoted her life to empowering Afghan youths, I am determined to get my bachelor’s in Medical Data science; create my own non-profit organization to support individuals from vulnerable countries who are passionate about AI and technology in leveraging its potentials for positive and ethical implementation in the world.
Experience

Extracurricular Activities

Volunteer Work

  • My commitment to community service began early and reached international recognition when, at the age of fifteen, I was nominated for the International Children’s Peace Prize 2021 in recognition of my voluntary efforts to educate and mentor Afghan women and children.
  • I started volunteer work at the Khadija Kubra Islamic Educational Center in 2019, where I provided educational support, mentoring, and tutoring to over 100 students in field of Islamic education.
  • Following the political changes in Afghanistan in 2021, I intensified my efforts to support Afghan women and children through various initiatives such as home-schooling programs and online tutoring sessions.
  • Although the situation in 2021 was challenging and courses were closing down, I remained consistent to support children education by organizing informal study groups and providing resources to ensure continued learning.
  • Despite the difficulties, my commitment to education and community service has remained unwavering. Right now, I am a volunteer English Instructor in Afghanistan Changemakers Org where I support Afghan women and children in their English language learning journey.

Jobs

  • From June 2021-August 2022, I worked as a English-Persian Medical Translator In Reviva Beauty Hospital Kabul, Afghanistan. My responsibilities included translating medical documents, patient information, and facilitating communication between English-speaking medical staff and Persian-speaking patients.
  • This experience allowed me to develop strong organizational and communication skills, as well as a deeper understanding of the healthcare system in Afghanistan.
  • Overall, my time at Reviva Beauty Hospital was a valuable learning experience that enhanced my language skills and provided me with insight into the medical field.
  • Subsequently, From September 2023-December 2025, I worked as an OPI (Over the Phone Interpreter) Medical Interpreter in Gaheez organization, where I provided real-time interpretation services for medical consultations over the phone. Through this experience, I further honed my language skills and gained a deeper understanding of medical terminology and healthcare settings.

Interships and Programs

  • My internship in Data-Sourcing by GAO-Tek org honed my skills in data collection, data cleaning, and data analysis.
  • I have participated in several biology projects; expanding my knowledge in biology, data science, and healthcare. During my studies in school, my curiosity in biology and its impact on world made me receive certificate of recognition for my biology project for 3 consecutive years.
  • I joined a 3- month program of AI and Data Science organised by UNDP and Horizon Bridge Consulting, where I explored how to make AI agents to further help in data tracking and data analysis and how artificial intelligence can be applied in healthcare system.
  • Addionally, I completed a 3-month workshop on Business and Intreprenuership with UCA and Acceralate Prosperity org, where I learned the fundamentals of business planning, marketing strategies, and financial management.
  • These experiences have equipped me with valuable skills and knowledge that I am eager to apply in my future academic and professional endeavors.
